This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Mobile Solution (SMS) (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in Kids Safe: Parent Control (“the App”). The App is designed to help a parent manage which apps can be opened during a time-limited Kids Mode session on the parent’s own Android phone.

1. Information and device access used by the App

Depending on the features you enable, the App may access or process the following information on the device:

  • Installed-app information: the names or package identifiers of installed apps may be read so the parent can choose which apps are allowed in a Kids Mode profile.
  • Foreground-app information: during an active Kids Mode session, the App uses Android usage-access capability to determine which app is currently in use and whether it is on the allowed list.
  • Session and profile settings: choices such as allowed apps, selected profile, session duration, and Parent PIN-related settings may be processed to provide the App's core functionality.
  • Permission and protection status: the App may check whether required Android permissions or special access settings are enabled.

These capabilities are used to provide the features described above. They should not be interpreted as a promise that the App can prevent every way of leaving or bypassing Kids Mode.

2. How information is used

Information accessed by the App is used to display installed apps for selection, apply the parent's allowed-app choices during a session, manage the session timer, show restriction screens for apps outside the allowlist, protect parent settings, and report permission status within the App.

4. Android permissions and special access

The App may request Android permissions or special access, including usage access to identify the foreground app during a Kids Mode session, display-over-other-apps access to show a restriction screen, and foreground-service capability to keep a user-started timed session running. The exact permissions requested depend on the App version and Android version. Android displays system prompts for special access where required.

The App uses these capabilities for its stated Kids Mode functionality. It does not provide remote control of a child's separate device in V1.

5. Children’s privacy and intended use

Kids Safe is a tool for a parent or guardian to configure and supervise Kids Mode on the parent's own phone when a child borrows it. The App is not a child account, does not pair with a separate child device, and does not provide cloud-based family management in V1. Parents or guardians should configure the allowed apps and supervise the child's use.
