KIDS SAFE: PARENT CONTROL

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 February 2022 · Last updated: 20 February 2022

EnglishTiếng Việt

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Mobile Solution (SMS) (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in Kids Safe: Parent Control (“the App”). The App helps a parent manage which apps can be opened during a time-limited Kids Mode session on the parent's own Android phone when a child borrows it. The App is intended for parents and adults; children may use the parent's device under the parent's supervision.

2. Information processed by the App

To provide its core features, the App accesses information about installed apps so the parent can choose an allowed-app list. During an active Kids Mode session, it uses Android usage access to identify the app currently in the foreground and enforce that list. This foreground-app check is performed only during an active session; the App does not retain a history of apps opened during sessions.

The App stores settings needed to operate Kids Mode, such as the allowed-app list, session duration and current session state. It also stores the Parent PIN in encrypted form. The App does not create child accounts or collect a child's name, date of birth, email address, phone number or profile photo. It does not provide cloud family pairing or remote management of a separate child's device.

When a Kids Mode session ends, the App deletes the session state and related session data. Changes to the allowed-app list replace the previous list; the App does not keep a history of changes. App data is stored locally on the device. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored app data through Android. The App does not use its own server to store or synchronize this information.

3. Analytics, crash reporting and app configuration

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics with their standard SDK behavior. These services may automatically process app usage, interaction, diagnostic, crash and technical information, such as app version and device or operating-system information, in accordance with Google's service settings and policies. We do not add custom analytics events, user identifiers, custom user properties, Crashlytics custom keys or custom logs.

The App uses Firebase Remote Config to retrieve configuration for enabling or disabling ad formats and controlling ad placements. It is not used to configure ad frequency or timing. We do not intentionally attach personal or device information to Remote Config requests; Google may process ordinary technical information needed to provide the service.

4. Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and may display banner, interstitial, app-open and native ads. The App does not intentionally add personal information or user identifiers to ad requests. The App is configured to request and display non-personalized ads. The SDK may still process technical and advertising-related information needed to deliver, measure and protect ads, in accordance with Google's applicable settings and policies.

Ads are disabled while Kids Mode is active and for users whose Premium entitlement is active. The App checks subscription status through Google Play Billing. The App stores the last successfully verified Premium status locally so it can be used until the next Billing check; it does not store purchase tokens or transaction identifiers. If the App is reinstalled or its data is cleared, it checks Google Play Billing again. If a subscription expires or is cancelled, the App updates the status when it next checks with Google Play.

The App uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to display a consent message to users and collect their privacy choices before advertising is served, as applicable.

5. Purchases

Monthly and yearly Premium subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google Play; SMS does not operate its own payment server and does not upload purchase transactions to its own server or Firebase. Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play. Please also review Google's applicable privacy information and payment terms.

6. Local processing and permissions

The App uses Android permissions and special access, including usage access, display-over-other-apps access and foreground-service capability, to run the parent-started Kids Mode session and show restrictions for apps that are not allowed. Installed-app information and foreground-app information are used for this purpose on the device and are not intentionally sent by the App to Firebase Analytics, Firebase Remote Config or AdMob.

The App does not intentionally collect GPS location, contacts, phone or SMS data, photos or videos, camera or microphone recordings, or device identifiers such as IMEI or Android ID. Third-party SDKs described in this Policy may nevertheless process technical or advertising-related information as explained above.

7. Sharing and third-party services

We do not operate our own backend to receive or synchronize Kids Mode settings or session activity. Information may be processed by Google services used in the App, including Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, Google Mobile Ads, Google UMP and Google Play Billing, for the purposes described in this Policy. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about advertising.

8. Children's privacy

Kids Safe is designed as a parent-facing tool for use on the parent's own device. It is intended for adults and parents; a child may use the device only under the parent's supervision. The App does not ask children to provide personal information and does not create child accounts. Parents are responsible for configuring and supervising Kids Mode.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can stop using the App or uninstall it to remove its locally stored app data. You can manage or cancel a Premium subscription through Google Play. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. Requests concerning information processed by Google may also need to be made directly to Google.

10. Data security and retention

The Parent PIN is encrypted before it is stored on the device. Other app settings and session information are stored locally to provide the App's features. Session-related data is deleted when the session ends. We do not maintain a cloud copy or session history on our own servers. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the App or our information practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Please review this page periodically.
