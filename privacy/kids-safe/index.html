1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Mobile Solution (SMS) (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in Kids Safe: Parent Control (“the App”). The App is designed to help a parent manage which apps can be opened during a time-limited Kids Mode session on the parent’s own Android phone when a child borrows it.

This Policy applies to information processed by the App and by SMS in connection with the App. It does not govern third-party apps, websites, services, or platforms that may be opened from the device or accessed independently. Those third parties are responsible for their own privacy practices; please review their policies where relevant. The App's V1 scope does not include a child account, remote management of a separate child's device, or cloud-based family pairing.

Please read this Policy together with any privacy information shown in the App or provided for a specific feature. If you do not agree with this Policy, please discontinue using the App.

2. Data controller and processor

For information processed by SMS in connection with the App, Smart Mobile Solution (SMS) determines the purposes and means of processing and acts as the data controller. Where we process information on behalf of another party, our role may instead be that of a data processor, as determined by the relevant arrangement and applicable law.

For questions about this Policy or the handling of information, contact us at help.mobilesolution@gmail.com.

3. Information and device access used by the App

Depending on the features you enable, the App may access or process the following information on the device:

  • Installed-app information: the names or package identifiers of installed apps may be read so the parent can choose which apps are allowed in a Kids Mode profile.
  • Foreground-app information: during an active Kids Mode session, the App uses Android usage-access capability to determine which app is currently in use and whether it is on the allowed list.
  • Session and profile settings: choices such as allowed apps, selected profile, session duration, and Parent PIN-related settings may be processed to provide the App's core functionality.
  • Permission and protection status: the App may check whether required Android permissions or special access settings are enabled.

These capabilities are used to provide the features described above. They should not be interpreted as a promise that the App can prevent every way of leaving or bypassing Kids Mode. We do not describe these device signals as a browsing history or as information collected for advertising purposes.

4. How information is used

Information accessed by the App is used to display installed apps for selection, apply the parent's allowed-app choices during a session, manage the session timer, show restriction screens for apps outside the allowlist, protect parent settings, and report permission status within the App.

We may also use information that you choose to send to us when you contact support to respond to your question, investigate the issue you report, and communicate with you. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your request.

6. Android permissions and special access

The App may request Android permissions or special access, including usage access to identify the foreground app during a Kids Mode session, display-over-other-apps access to show a restriction screen, access to installed-app information to let the parent configure an allowlist, and foreground-service capability to keep a user-started timed session running. The exact permissions requested depend on the App version and Android version. Android displays system prompts for special access where required.

The App uses these capabilities for its stated Kids Mode functionality. It does not provide remote control of a child's separate device in V1.
