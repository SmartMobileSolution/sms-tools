Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 February 2022

Pictarow — by Smart Mobile Solution ("SMS", "we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and your choices. In short: your photos and table contents are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers. Optional iCloud Sync is available on iOS for Premium users. Android version 1 does not currently offer cloud backup or synchronization. We do not provide user accounts.

1. Data that stays on your device

When you scan or import a table, the app processes:

  • the photo or image you choose;
  • the text recognized from it using on-device recognition technology;
  • the table cells you edit, and file names you create when exporting.

All recognition happens on your device. This content is stored locally in the app and is not sent to our servers or to any third-party OCR service. We do not have accounts, so we cannot identify you or access your tables.

Exported files (XLSX, CSV) are created on your device. Where they go next (Files, email, other apps) is your choice and subject to those apps' policies.

2. Cloud sync and backup

iOS: Optional iCloud Sync is available to Premium users. If you enable it, supported library data is stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit and can sync across your supported devices. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to that data.

Android: Cloud backup and synchronization are not available in Android version 1. Your supported app data remains stored locally on your device. If cloud backup or synchronization is introduced in a future Android version, this policy will be updated to describe the service and data involved before that feature is made available.

3. Information collected automatically

We use Google Firebase services to understand app stability and usage:

  • Firebase Analytics: app interactions (for example, which screens or features are used) and a device/app instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and performance data (device model, OS version, app version, stack traces).
  • Firebase Remote Config: fetches app settings (for example, ad configuration).

These Firebase services are used in both the iOS and Android versions of Pictarow.

We do not put your images, recognized text, cell values or file names into analytics, crash reports or logs. This data is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Depending on your region and choices:

  • A consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices where required, such as in the EEA, UK and other regions.
  • On iOS, App Tracking Transparency is used to request permission before tracking as required by Apple's platform rules. Android uses Google's applicable advertising and privacy controls.
  • Ad partners may collect your device identifier, IP address, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads.

You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options (when shown), and, on iOS, in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Premium removes all ads.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by the applicable app store: Apple App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. Payments are handled by Apple or Google, and we do not receive or store your payment card or payment account details. The app checks subscription entitlement using the platform's billing system (StoreKit on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android).

7. Retention and deletion

Your tables and images remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. On iOS, copies synchronized to iCloud are subject to your iCloud settings and can be managed through the app and your Apple account settings. Android version 1 does not create cloud backups or synchronized copies. Analytics, crash and advertising data handled by Google services is retained under the applicable service settings and policies. We do not store your table content on our own servers.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise a right, email help.mobilesolution@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell personal information.

9. International transfers

Firebase and AdMob may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Their transfers rely on safeguards described in Google's policies.

10. Children

Pictarow is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Security

Data stored locally is protected by the security features of your device and operating system. Data transmitted to the third-party services described above is protected in transit using the safeguards provided by those services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be noted in the app or on this page.
