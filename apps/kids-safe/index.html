APP PREVIEW

A closer look at Kids Safe.

APP FEATURES

Simple controls for shared-phone moments.

01

Choose allowed apps

Review the apps installed on your phone and select which ones can be opened during Kids Mode.

02

Set a time limit

Start a Kids Mode session with a duration you choose for the time your child borrows your phone.

03

Protect your setup with a Parent PIN

Use a Parent PIN as part of managing your Kids Safe settings and session.

HOW IT WORKS

Set up a session, then hand over your phone.

STEP 01

Choose the apps

Select the apps you want to make available during the session.

STEP 02

Start Kids Mode

Choose a session duration and start Kids Mode before your child borrows the phone.

STEP 03

End the session

When the session ends, Kids Safe clears the session state and related session data.

DESIGNED FOR YOUR PHONE

Parent-led control.
On one device.

Kids Safe is designed for a parent to configure and supervise Kids Mode on the parent's own Android phone when a child borrows it.

✓Use an allowed-app list on the parent's phone
✓Run a time-limited Kids Mode session
✓No separate child-device remote management or cloud family pairing in V1
